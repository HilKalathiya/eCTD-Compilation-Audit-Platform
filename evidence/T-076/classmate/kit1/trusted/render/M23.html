2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 36 months when stored at 20°C to 25°C. Temperature excursions between 15°C and 30°C are permitted. Data are available for 36 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 99.28% label claim to 100.00% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under long-term conditions ranged from 0.050% area to 0.122% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution (15 min) under long-term conditions ranged from 94.92% dissolved to 96.00% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.
