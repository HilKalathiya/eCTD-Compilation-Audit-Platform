2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 30 months when stored at 15°C to 30°C. Excursions are permitted from 2°C to 30°C. Stability was demonstrated for 24 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under long-term conditions ranged from 94.06% dissolved to 97.82% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under intermediate conditions ranged from 0.071% area to 0.226% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under accelerated conditions ranged from 99.25% label claim to 101.40% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under accelerated conditions ranged from 0.070% area to 0.188% area.
