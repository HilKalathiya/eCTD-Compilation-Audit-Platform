2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 30 months when stored at 2°C to 25°C. Excursions are permitted from 5°C to 40°C. Stability was demonstrated for 18 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under long-term conditions ranged from 91.89% dissolved to 95.15% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under intermediate conditions ranged from 98.72% label claim to 100.44% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under intermediate conditions ranged from 95.32% dissolved to 95.87% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under accelerated conditions ranged from 98.01% label claim to 101.19% label claim.
