2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 48 months when stored at 15°C to 30°C. Excursions are permitted from 10°C to 35°C. Stability was demonstrated for 48 months at long-term conditions and 3 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under intermediate conditions ranged from 90.36% dissolved to 90.76% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under accelerated conditions ranged from 99.25% label claim to 101.40% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under accelerated conditions ranged from 0.143% area to 0.238% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under accelerated conditions ranged from 91.65% dissolved to 96.92% dissolved.
