2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 24 months when stored at 15°C to 30°C. Excursions are permitted from 10°C to 35°C. Stability was demonstrated for 24 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under intermediate conditions ranged from 0.132% area to 0.234% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under intermediate conditions ranged from 91.80% dissolved to 96.91% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under accelerated conditions ranged from 90.27% dissolved to 97.44% dissolved.
