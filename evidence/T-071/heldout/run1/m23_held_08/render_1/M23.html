2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 48 months when stored at 20°C to 25°C. Excursions are permitted from 10°C to 35°C. Stability was demonstrated for 48 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under long-term conditions ranged from 91.22% dissolved to 94.47% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under accelerated conditions ranged from 0.058% area to 0.221% area.
