2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 48 months when stored at 2°C to 25°C. Excursions are permitted from 2°C to 40°C. Stability was demonstrated for 48 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under intermediate conditions ranged from 91.11% dissolved to 97.77% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under accelerated conditions ranged from 91.18% dissolved to 96.51% dissolved.
