2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 36 months when stored at 15°C to 25°C. Excursions are permitted from 10°C to 35°C. Stability was demonstrated for 36 months at long-term conditions and 3 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under long-term conditions ranged from 90.45% dissolved to 92.10% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under intermediate conditions ranged from 91.66% dissolved to 96.87% dissolved.
