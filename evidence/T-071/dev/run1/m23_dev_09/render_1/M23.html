2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 18 months when stored at 15°C to 25°C. Excursions are permitted from 0°C to 40°C. Stability was demonstrated for 18 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under intermediate conditions ranged from 91.08% dissolved to 95.94% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under accelerated conditions ranged from 0.107% area to 0.246% area.
