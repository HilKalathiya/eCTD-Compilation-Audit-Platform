2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 36 months when stored at 2°C to 25°C. Excursions are permitted from 10°C to 35°C. Stability was demonstrated for 18 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 98.26% label claim to 100.34% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under intermediate conditions ranged from 98.24% label claim to 100.80% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under intermediate conditions ranged from 0.088% area to 0.246% area.
