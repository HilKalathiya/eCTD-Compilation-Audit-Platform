2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 30 months when stored at 15°C to 25°C. Excursions are permitted from 0°C to 40°C. Stability was demonstrated for 24 months at long-term conditions and 3 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under intermediate conditions ranged from 98.56% label claim to 101.41% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under intermediate conditions ranged from 0.065% area to 0.223% area.
