2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 48 months when stored at 20°C to 25°C. Excursions are permitted from 5°C to 40°C. Stability was demonstrated for 48 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 99.00% label claim to 101.10% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under intermediate conditions ranged from 91.26% dissolved to 96.95% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under accelerated conditions ranged from 98.18% label claim to 101.47% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution under accelerated conditions ranged from 91.45% dissolved to 96.07% dissolved.
