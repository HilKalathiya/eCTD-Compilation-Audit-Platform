2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 30 months when stored at 5°C to 25°C. Permitted temperature excursions range from 5°C to 40°C. Stability was demonstrated for 48 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 98.29% label claim to 101.17% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, under intermediate conditions, Dissolution (15 min) ranged from 92.45% dissolved to 95.68% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under intermediate conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under accelerated conditions ranged from 94.50% label claim to 98.53% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under accelerated conditions had results outside the limit.
