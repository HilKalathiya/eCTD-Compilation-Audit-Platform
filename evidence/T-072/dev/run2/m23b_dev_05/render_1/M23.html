2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 24 months when stored at 2°C to 25°C. Permitted temperature excursions range from 2°C to 30°C. Stability was demonstrated for 48 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, under long-term conditions, Dissolution (15 min) ranged from 90.49% dissolved to 97.40% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under accelerated conditions ranged from 0.194% area to 0.242% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.
