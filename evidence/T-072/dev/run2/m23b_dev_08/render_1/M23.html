2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 24 months when stored at 15°C to 25°C. Permitted temperature excursions range from 5°C to 40°C. Stability was demonstrated for 24 months at long-term conditions and 3 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 98.52% label claim to 101.46% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, under long-term conditions, Dissolution (15 min) ranged from 90.76% dissolved to 97.86% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under intermediate conditions ranged from 99.53% label claim to 100.90% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under intermediate conditions was outside the limit.
