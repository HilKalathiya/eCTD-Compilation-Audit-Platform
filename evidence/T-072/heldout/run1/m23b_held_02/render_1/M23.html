2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 18 months when stored at 15°C to 30°C. Permitted temperature excursions range from 0°C to 30°C. Stability was demonstrated for 36 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under long-term conditions ranged from 0.122% area to 0.187% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, under intermediate conditions, Dissolution (15 min) ranged from 93.43% dissolved to 97.86% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under intermediate conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under accelerated conditions ranged from 99.37% label claim to 100.39% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under accelerated conditions ranged from 0.077% area to 0.187% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.
