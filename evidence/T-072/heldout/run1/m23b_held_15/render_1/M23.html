2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 30 months when stored at 2°C to 25°C. Permitted temperature excursions range from 2°C to 30°C. Stability was demonstrated for 18 months at long-term conditions and 3 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 94.50% label claim to 101.46% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions had results outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under long-term conditions ranged from 0.071% area to 0.222% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, under accelerated conditions, Dissolution (15 min) ranged from 94.01% dissolved to 97.28% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.
