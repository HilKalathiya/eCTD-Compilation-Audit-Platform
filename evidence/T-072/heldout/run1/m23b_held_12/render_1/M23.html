2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 12 months when stored at 20°C to 25°C. Permitted temperature excursions range from 0°C to 30°C. Stability was demonstrated for 12 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, under long-term conditions, Dissolution (15 min) ranged from 91.01% dissolved to 97.96% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under accelerated conditions ranged from 98.10% label claim to 100.63% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under accelerated conditions ranged from 0.139% area to 0.520% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under accelerated conditions had results outside the limit.
