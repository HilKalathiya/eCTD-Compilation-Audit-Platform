2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 36 months when stored at 5°C to 25°C. Permitted temperature excursions range from 5°C to 30°C. Stability was demonstrated for 36 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under long-term conditions ranged from 0.059% area to 0.211% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, under accelerated conditions, Dissolution (15 min) ranged from 96.56% dissolved to 98.00% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.
