2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 24 months when stored at 20°C to 25°C. Permitted temperature excursions range from 0°C to 30°C. Stability was demonstrated for 36 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 99.15% label claim to 100.49% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, under long-term conditions, Dissolution (15 min) ranged from 78.50% dissolved to 95.30% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution (15 min) under long-term conditions had results outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under accelerated conditions ranged from 98.21% label claim to 100.25% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, under accelerated conditions, Dissolution (15 min) ranged from 90.36% dissolved to 95.87% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.
