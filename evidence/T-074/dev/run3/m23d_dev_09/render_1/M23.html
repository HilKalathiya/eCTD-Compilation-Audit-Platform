2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 30 months when stored at 20°C to 25°C. Temperature excursions between 5°C and 40°C are permitted. Data are available for 48 months at long-term conditions and 3 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 98.31% label claim to 101.36% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under long-term conditions ranged from 0.057% area to 0.445% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.
