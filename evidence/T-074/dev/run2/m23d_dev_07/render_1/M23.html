2.3.P.8 Stability

HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED: a stored result is outside its limit (long-term Degradation product). No shelf life is proposed until this is reviewed.

Temperature excursions between 0°C and 40°C are permitted. Data are available for 12 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under long-term conditions ranged from 0.054% area to 0.320% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under long-term conditions had results outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution (15 min) under long-term conditions ranged from 90.02% dissolved to 97.89% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under accelerated conditions ranged from 0.050% area to 0.155% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution (15 min) under accelerated conditions ranged from 94.41% dissolved to 94.93% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.
