2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 48 months when stored at 10°C to 30°C. Temperature excursions between 2°C and 30°C are permitted. Data are available for 36 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 98.26% label claim to 101.35% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution (15 min) under long-term conditions ranged from 91.30% dissolved to 95.79% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.
