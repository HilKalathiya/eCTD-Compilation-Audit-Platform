2.3.P.8 Stability

HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED: a stored result is outside its limit (long-term Assay). No shelf life is proposed until this is reviewed.

Temperature excursions between 0°C and 30°C are permitted. Data are available for 18 months at long-term conditions and 3 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 94.50% label claim to 99.07% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions had results outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under long-term conditions ranged from 0.185% area to 0.966% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution (15 min) under long-term conditions ranged from 90.14% dissolved to 97.78% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.
