2.3.P.8 Stability

HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED: a stored result is outside its limit (long-term Dissolution). No shelf life is proposed until this is reviewed.

Temperature excursions between 5°C and 40°C are permitted. Data are available for 18 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 98.60% label claim to 101.38% label claim.
