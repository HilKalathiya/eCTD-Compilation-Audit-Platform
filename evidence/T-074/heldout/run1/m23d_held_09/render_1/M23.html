2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 30 months when stored at 10°C to 30°C. Temperature excursions between 0°C and 40°C are permitted. Data are available for 36 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 98.63% label claim to 101.17% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under accelerated conditions ranged from 96.97% label claim to 104.00% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.
