2.3.P.8 Stability

HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED: a stored result is outside its limit (intermediate Degradation product). No shelf life is proposed until this is reviewed.

Temperature excursions between 2°C and 30°C are permitted. Data are available for 24 months at long-term conditions and 3 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 99.64% label claim to 100.30% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under long-term conditions ranged from 0.176% area to 0.560% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under intermediate conditions ranged from 0.203% area to 1.020% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under intermediate conditions had results outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under accelerated conditions ranged from 0.041% area to 0.654% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.
