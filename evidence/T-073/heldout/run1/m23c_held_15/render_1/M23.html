2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 12 months when stored at 10°C to 30°C. Temperature excursions between 10°C and 35°C are permitted. Data are available for 12 months at long-term conditions and 3 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 98.86% label claim to 100.73% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under long-term conditions ranged from 0.078% area to 0.210% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under accelerated conditions ranged from 96.53% label claim to 104.00% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under accelerated conditions ranged from 0.107% area to 0.154% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.
