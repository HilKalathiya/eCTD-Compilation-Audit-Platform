2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 24 months when stored at 10°C to 30°C. Temperature excursions between 10°C and 35°C are permitted. Stability data cover 48 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution (15 min) under long-term conditions ranged from 72.50% dissolved to 91.78% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution (15 min) under long-term conditions had results outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution (15 min) under accelerated conditions ranged from 91.13% dissolved to 97.33% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.
