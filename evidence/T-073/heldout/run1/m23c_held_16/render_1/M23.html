2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 36 months when stored at 2°C to 25°C. Temperature excursions between 0°C and 30°C are permitted. Data are available for 36 months at long-term conditions and 3 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 98.27% label claim to 101.27% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under long-term conditions ranged from 0.096% area to 0.238% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution (15 min) under long-term conditions ranged from 90.02% dissolved to 97.01% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under accelerated conditions ranged from 98.85% label claim to 101.49% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under accelerated conditions ranged from 0.223% area to 0.520% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under accelerated conditions had results outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution (15 min) under accelerated conditions ranged from 94.88% dissolved to 97.31% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.
