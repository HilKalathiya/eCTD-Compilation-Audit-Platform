2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 60 months when stored at 10°C to 30°C. Temperature excursions between 2°C and 30°C are permitted. Data are available for 48 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 98.55% label claim to 100.72% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under long-term conditions ranged from 0.120% area to 0.250% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.
