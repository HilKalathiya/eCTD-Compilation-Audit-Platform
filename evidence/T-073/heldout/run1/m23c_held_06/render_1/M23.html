2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 24 months when stored at 2°C to 25°C. Temperature excursions between 0°C and 30°C are permitted. Data are available for 24 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 98.27% label claim to 99.84% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under long-term conditions ranged from 0.086% area to 0.231% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution (15 min) under long-term conditions ranged from 91.68% dissolved to 97.77% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Dissolution (15 min) result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under accelerated conditions ranged from 98.93% label claim to 101.33% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Degradation product under accelerated conditions ranged from 0.104% area to 0.233% area.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Degradation product result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution (15 min) under accelerated conditions ranged from 67.50% dissolved to 96.04% dissolved.

In illustrative demonstration data, Dissolution (15 min) under accelerated conditions had results outside the limit.
