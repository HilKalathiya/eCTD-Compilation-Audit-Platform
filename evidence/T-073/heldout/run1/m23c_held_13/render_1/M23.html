2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 12 months when stored at 15°C to 30°C. Temperature excursions between 0°C and 40°C are permitted. Stability data cover 12 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 94.50% label claim to 100.63% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions had results outside the limit.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under accelerated conditions ranged from 98.64% label claim to 101.04% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under accelerated conditions was outside the limit.
