2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 48 months when stored at 15°C to 30°C. Temperature excursions between 2°C and 30°C are permitted. Data are available for 36 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 99.45% label claim to 101.20% label claim.
