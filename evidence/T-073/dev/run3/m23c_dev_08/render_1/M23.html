2.3.P.8 Stability

The proposed shelf life is 24 months when stored at 2°C to 25°C. Temperature excursions between 0°C and 30°C are permitted. Data are available for 18 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.

In illustrative demonstration data, Assay under long-term conditions ranged from 98.91% label claim to 101.45% label claim.

In illustrative demonstration data, no Assay result under long-term conditions was outside the limit.
