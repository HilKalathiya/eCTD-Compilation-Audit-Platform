### 3.2.P.5.6 Justification of the acceptance criterion: Impurity L

The acceptance criterion for Impurity L is not more than 0.5%, set at the ICH Q3B(R2) qualification threshold for an assumed maximum daily dose of 40 mg: “Qualification Thresholds Maximum Daily Dose1 Threshold2,3 < 10 mg 1.0% or 50 µg TDI, whichever is lower 10 mg - 100 mg 0.5% or 200 µg TDI, whichever is lower” (ICH Q3B(R2), Step 4 2006-06-02, page 11). Results for 21 batches ranged from 0.05% to 0.11%. No result was outside the limit.

| Attribute | Limit | Basis | n | min | max | outside limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Impurity L | NMT 0.5% | Q3B qualification threshold, assumed dose 40 mg | 21 | 0.05% | 0.11% | 0 |

_The values, the limit direction, the basis and the quoted Q3B text were written by code from stored facts; the model wrote only the words._
