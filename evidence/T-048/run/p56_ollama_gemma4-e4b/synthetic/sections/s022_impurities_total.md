### 3.2.P.5.6 Justification of the acceptance criterion: Total impurities

The acceptance criterion for Total impurities is not more than 0.07%, set at declared by the author for this demonstration, with no public source. Results for 26 batches ranged from 0.05% to 0.29%. 15 batches of 26 batches results were outside the limit.

| Attribute | Limit | Basis | n | min | max | outside limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total impurities | NMT 0.07% | declared (no public source) | 26 | 0.05% | 0.29% | 15 |

_The values, the limit direction, the basis and the quoted Q3B text were written by code from stored facts; the model wrote only the words._
