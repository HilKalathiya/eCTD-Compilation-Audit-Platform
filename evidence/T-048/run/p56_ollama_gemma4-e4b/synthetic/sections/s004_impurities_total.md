### 3.2.P.5.6 Justification of the acceptance criterion: Total impurities

The acceptance criterion for Total impurities is not more than 0.06%, which is declared by the author for this demonstration, with no public source. Results for 25 batches ranged from 0.05% to 0.32%. 17 batches of 25 batches results were outside the limit.

| Attribute | Limit | Basis | n | min | max | outside limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total impurities | NMT 0.06% | declared (no public source) | 25 | 0.05% | 0.32% | 17 |

_The values, the limit direction, the basis and the quoted Q3B text were written by code from stored facts; the model wrote only the words._
