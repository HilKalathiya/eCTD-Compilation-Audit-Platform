### 3.2.P.5.6 Justification of the acceptance criterion: Total impurities

The acceptance criterion for Total impurities is not more than 0.21%, which is declared by the author for this demonstration, with no public source. Results for 22 batches ranged from 0.05% to 0.29%. six batches of 22 batches results were outside the limit.

| Attribute | Limit | Basis | n | min | max | outside limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total impurities | NMT 0.21% | declared (no public source) | 22 | 0.05% | 0.29% | 6 |
