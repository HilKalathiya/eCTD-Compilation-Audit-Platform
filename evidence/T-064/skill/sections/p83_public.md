### 3.2.P.8.3 Stability: public facts

The proposed shelf life is 36 months when stored at 20°C to 25°C. Excursions are permitted from 15°C to 30°C. Stability was demonstrated for 36 months at long-term conditions and 6 months at accelerated conditions.
